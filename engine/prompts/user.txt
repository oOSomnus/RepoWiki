The page must explain the module's role in the system, its important
interfaces, and a real control or data flow tied to the supplied source.
Explain the responsibility boundary, connect important types/functions in
prose, and cover state/lifecycle, dependencies, and error/resource behavior
when evidenced. Use at least two distinct source path or symbol anchors when
the supplied component set contains at least two components. Use several
substantive paragraphs across at least two semantic areas; do not pad the page
to meet a length target. Use a free-form structure appropriate to the module.
Do not produce a directory listing, component inventory, repeated template,
or generic explanation.

The reference examples are supplied in full, not as excerpts. Use their
information density and explanation style, but derive every fact from the
target repository's evidence and write original prose.

Add Mermaid only when it makes the architecture easier to understand. Prefer
concrete module/interface/storage names and relationships from the supplied
context. Do not invent a Caller -> Entry -> Core -> Result graph merely to
include a diagram. Link to related documented pages when the module tree
provides them.
